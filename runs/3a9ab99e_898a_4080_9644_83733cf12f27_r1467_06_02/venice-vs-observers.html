Your Venice game against the observer runs

Venice, played, 1342–1515 (39 autosaves, branch location-profiles), against five observer runs of the stockpile branch (77768726, 16fe282a, ebdb631e, cf884236, 97f9d39a; 1342–1837, one save every five years). The runs are read at your save dates. The question: what did your Venice change, and what moved without it?

You
You broke the Ottomans.

Constantinople is Venetian from about 1423 and Crete from about 1450. In 1512 the Ottomans hold 338 locations (runs 437–637), only 52 of them in the Balkans (117–227). Hungary fills the gap with 154 Balkan locations (50–111). The Balkans end with less control and fewer people than in any run.

You
You moved Europe's trade.

From 1450 Europe exports 25–32 % more than in every run, and imports a larger share of its goods (12 % against 9–11 %). Italy exports about twice the run median, the Balkans too.

Not you
The world economy moved without you.

Population stays inside the run range all game. Tax base ends 12–17 % below every run from 1472, and three quarters of that gap lies outside Europe. The whole world is cheaper and better stocked: lower prices, more goods in stock, less unmet pop demand, fuller food stores, 40 % fewer slaves from the start. That points to the build or the game rules, not to Venice.

Maybe you
Europe lags on towns and ideas.

Without Venice, Europe has 8–10 % fewer burghers, 5 % fewer towns, more subsistence peasants, and from about 1490 fewer institutions per person and lower literacy. Outside Europe literacy is higher than in the runs, so this is Europe's own. Clergy is low from the very start, so at least that part is the build.

Venice in 1512

The AI's Venice loses the city of Venice in all five runs: to Aquileia (77768726, 16fe282a), Verona (cf884236), Bologna (97f9d39a) or Hungary (ebdb631e, last of them).

What to keep in mind

  • One played game against five observer runs, and not the same build. Your game runs on location-profiles with the EU5 1.4.8 beta port, the building price change of 6 November, start stock only for goods a market makes, the Mining Charter and integration changes. Its game rules use historical AI personalities.
  • Your autosaves fall in June, July or October, the runs' in April. Food stores are compared on spring saves only (March to June).
  • Regions are EU5 super-regions and regions by location. "Europe without Venice" drops the locations of Venice and its subjects. Market figures (exports, prices, stock) go by the region of each market's centre.
  • The causes of the world-wide shift are not traced yet.
